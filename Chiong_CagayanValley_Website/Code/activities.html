Activities Experiences

Adventure

  • Explore the chambers of Callao Cave
  • Take a boat ride on the Pinacanauan River in Peñablanca
  • Hike to the Cape Engaño Lighthouse on Palaui Island
  • Bike across the hills of Batanes

Culture

  • Walk through the stone houses of Chavayan in Sabtang
  • Visit St. Peter Metropolitan Cathedral in Tuguegarao
  • Watch the Bambanti Festival street dancing in Ilagan
  • Meet Ivatan weavers who make the vakul

Food

  • Eat pancit batil patong in Tuguegarao
  • Buy Tuguegarao longganisa to take home
  • Try uvud balls, a banana-stem dish from Batanes
  • Taste fresh seafood in Santa Ana

Relax in Nature

  • Swim in the pools of Pinzal Falls
  • Picnic at Fuyot Springs National Park
  • Watch the sunset at Magat Dam
  • Swim at Anguib Beach in Santa Ana
