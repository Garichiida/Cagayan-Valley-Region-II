3-Day Itinerary from Tuguegarao

Day Morning Afternoon Evening
Day 1 Arrive in Tuguegarao, visit St. Peter Metropolitan Cathedral Callao Cave and the Pinacanauan River Dinner with pancit batil patong
Day 2 Drive to Santa Ana, boat to Palaui Island Hike to Cape Engaño Lighthouse, swim at Anguib Beach Seafood dinner in Santa Ana
Day 3 Walk along the Cagayan River in Tuguegarao Buy longganisa and souvenirs Depart

Travel Tips

  • Visit between March and May for the driest weather.
  • Book boats to Palaui Island early in the morning, since waves get rougher later.
  • Batanes is reached by plane from Manila or Tuguegarao. Book ahead.
  • Bring cash. Many rural areas do not have ATMs.
